Hi, I'm Maisha! I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at the University of Texas at Arlington. I work in the Heracleia Human-Centered Computing Lab under the supervision of Dr. Fillia Makedon. My research lies in human-centered computing (HCC) and human-robot interaction (HRI), with a focus on integrating large language models (LLMs), vision language models(VLMs) in assistive and collaborative technologies.

My recent projects include NavVI, a telerobotic simulation with multimodal feedback for visually impaired navigation and Mindful Assistant, a personalized adaptive robotic system for cognitive fatigue assessment in individuals with paralysis.

Outside of research, I love being in the mountains - hiking, breathing in the views, and getting happily lost on trails. I also enjoy exploring foods from different cultures around the world.
